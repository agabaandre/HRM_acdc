# Tasks & AD Manager Hub Polish — Design

**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Status:** Approved  
**Scope:** Hub pages only (`/tasks`, `/admanager`). List pages unchanged.

## Approach

Enrich hub APIs with `summary` KPIs and richer `links` (icon, description, count). Frontend cards match Performance/Payroll KPI + outlined nav cards.

## Tasks hub

- KPIs: total, pending, overdue, execution_rate (weekly stats defaults)
- Links: Weekly tasks, Workplan activities

## AD manager hub

- KPIs: to_disable, disabled
- Links: Accounts to disable, Disabled accounts
