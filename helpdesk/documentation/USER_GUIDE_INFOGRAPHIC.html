Getting started

Sign in & open Helpdesk

Use your existing Staff portal session — no separate password for Helpdesk.

  1. 1
    Go to the Staff portal Sign in at your usual Africa CDC Staff home (same login as Finance and APM).
  2. 2
    Open IT Service Desk From the home dashboard, choose IT Service Desk (Helpdesk).
  3. 3
    Explore the home page Browse FAQs, try Ask Helpdesk (AI assistant), or jump straight to New request.
Tip: If you see “No active session”, return to the Staff portal and open Helpdesk from there.

Log a request

Create a new ticket

Describe the issue clearly so the right team can help quickly.

  1. 1
    Click New request Available from the home hero or the Tickets page.
  2. 2
    Choose a category Pick the issue type (e.g. email, hardware, access). No category is pre-selected — you must choose one.
  3. 3
    Describe the problem Include what happened, when it started, error messages, and your device or location.
  4. 4
    Submit By default the ticket is for you. Tick “another staff member” only if logging on someone else’s behalf.
Tip: Try Ask Helpdesk first for instant answers from the knowledge base.

Track & follow up

Follow your ticket

Stay updated from the Tickets list and ticket detail page.

  1. 1
    Open Tickets Search by ticket number, subject, requester, assignee, or status.
  2. 2
    Read updates & add comments Agents reply on the ticket thread. Add more detail anytime from the detail page.
  3. 3
    Check status Statuses include Open, In progress, Awaiting confirm, Resolved, and Closed.
  4. 4
    Not fixed yet? On a closed ticket you can comment and reopen so the assignee is notified by email.
Tip: You receive email when an agent resolves your ticket or replies to your comment.

For support agents

Agent desk & resolution

How IT staff pick up, work, and close requests.

  1. 1
    Agent dashboard Open Agent desk to see assigned work, queues, and workload.
  2. 2
    Reply & reassign Post public comments for the requester or internal notes for the team. Reassign when another agent should own the ticket.
  3. 3
    Submit resolution Document what was fixed, then close the ticket. Optionally publish the fix to the knowledge base.
  4. 4
    Live screen dashboard Open /screen on a TV or lobby display for real-time queue stats (no personal data shown).
Admins: Use Settings for categories, agents, SLA rules, and integrations (WhatsApp / Teams).
